The Loadout / Privacy

Privacy

Why we care: plate-reading cameras on every corner, apps that sell where you’ve been, ads that follow you from site to site. A lot of the internet now runs on watching people. We cover AI and tech, so we see how that data gets collected and used. This site isn’t going to add to it.

The short version

  • This site has no analytics, no ads, no trackers and no cookies, and it doesn’t load anything from other companies. Even the fonts are hosted here.
  • We never sell, rent, share or send your email or personal info to anyone unless you’ve explicitly said it’s okay. Period.
  • The one thing we can’t switch off: our web host, GitHub, logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. More on that below.

What this site collects

Nothing, from us. There are no sign-up forms, comment boxes or tracking scripts here. The only thing the site saves is your light/dark mode choice, which stays in your own browser (in something called localStorage). It’s never sent anywhere, and clearing your browser data wipes it.

Our web host: GitHub

theloadout.fyi is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says that when anyone visits a GitHub Pages site, “the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes.” That happens on GitHub’s servers, we don’t see those logs, and there’s no setting that lets us turn it off. GitHub explains how it handles that data in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you subscribe: Substack

The Loadout is sent through Substack. The subscribe buttons here are plain links to Substack’s sign-up page. Nothing from Substack loads on this site until you click one. Once you’re on Substack, you’re on Substack’s site. It uses its own cookies and analytics, including Google’s ad-measurement tags, all under the Substack Privacy Policy.

When you sign up, Substack stores your email and sends you each issue. In our Substack dashboard, we can see:

  • your email address (and your name, if you add one)
  • a rough location (country and state or province)
  • when you subscribed and how you found us
  • whether you open our emails and which links you click

Opens are counted with a tiny image in each email. If you block images, or your mail app hides opens (Apple Mail does), we won’t see them.

We use all of this for one thing: sending you The Loadout and the occasional message from us, like a correction or a heads-up about the newsletter. We look at opens and clicks only to see which stories land. Substack is its own company. Under its policy, it can also email you about Substack itself, and you can opt out of those from the emails themselves.

Leaving, or asking us to delete your info

  • Unsubscribe: every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. One click and you’re off the list.
  • Delete your info from our list: reply to any issue, or email theloadoutai@substack.com, and we’ll remove you from our subscriber list.
  • Your Substack account: Substack holds that, not us. You can delete it in your Substack account settings, or contact Substack at privacy@substackinc.com.

Links to other sites

We link out to Substack, YouTube, X, Instagram, GitHub and the sources in our stories. Once you click, that site’s rules apply. We set our links up so the other site only sees that you came from theloadout.fyi, not which page you were on.

If this ever changes

If we ever add anything that collects info, we’ll update this page first and tell you in the newsletter.
